"Database Systems: Conquering Normalization Anomalies and the Hectic Final Manual Submissions"

The final week of the second semester at UET Lahore, Faisalabad Campus, was undoubtedly the most hectic and physically draining period of my entire academic journey. Our final examination presented an incredibly technical challenge, featuring a massive 20-mark question entirely dedicated to complex database normalization, identifying structural anomalies, and solving dense Relational Algebra equations. Sitting in that exam hall, meticulously breaking down complex functional dependencies and systematically eliminating insertion, deletion, and modification anomalies demanded absolute cognitive clarity under intense pressure.

![Desktop View](/assets/img/SS1.jpg)

Adding to the intense pressure of the final examinations was a massive backlog of practical documentation. Because we had not consistently submitted our laboratory manuals throughout the busy semester, we were hit with the monumental task of compiling, formatting, and verifying 14 highly detailed database manuals all at once during finals week. This grueling process required us to compile hundreds of complex MySQL queries, ERD diagrams, and optimization logs into pristine, professional engineering reports while simultaneously studying for our theoretical papers.

![Desktop View](/assets/img/SS2.jpg)

This exhausting final trial was a powerful lesson in accountability, discipline, and consistent project management. Looking back at the immense pressure of that final week, I can clearly see how much my technical endurance and engineering mindset have evolved under the rigorous standards maintained by Dr. Bilal Ahmad. Surviving this final academic crunch and successfully publishing my complete journey on this GitHub portfolio marks a definitive turning point in my academic career, proving that I possess the grit, technical precision, and dedication required to excel in the field of Computer Engineering.
